## Planning Controls

The site is zoned R4 High Density Residential under the Parramatta Local
Environmental Plan. The maximum building height for the lot is 21 metres. The
floor space ratio (FSR) limit applicable to the parcel is 2.4:1.

## Setbacks

The minimum front setback is 6 metres from the primary street boundary. Side
setbacks are 3 metres at ground level, increasing to 6 metres above the fourth
storey. The rear setback is 8 metres.

## Bushfire and Flood

The parcel is not bushfire prone. The north-eastern corner of the site sits
within the 1-in-100-year flood planning area, requiring a minimum habitable
floor level of RL 9.2 metres AHD.

## Heritage

No heritage items are listed on the site. A locally listed heritage item exists
two lots to the south, so view-corridor and overshadowing impacts should be
assessed at development application stage.

## Indicative Development Costs

The indicative development cost for an 8-storey residential flat building is
estimated at 42 million dollars, excluding land acquisition and finance costs.
Estimated gross realisable value is 61 million dollars.
